# Roof variety edition

Run `launch.py`, or open http://127.0.0.1:47845/ while its server is running. Search and generate areas as in version 2. This edition keeps its own custom-area cache, keyed by the new renderer hashes, so old monochrome renders cannot silently replace the new finishes. Version 2 remains available on 47844.

Mapped roof materials take precedence over shape/class heuristics, including industrial inference. Supported material families include slate, clay/ceramic tiles, metals, asphalt/bitumen and generic flat concrete/gravel/membrane surfaces (the last group uses an approximate asphalt surface). Unsupported material tags are recorded in the finish report. Mapped colours support hexadecimal RGB and a small named-colour palette. Colours recolour the source texture's luminance while retaining surface detail and normal maps; the result remains an approximation under physical lighting, not colour-calibrated photography.

Where attributes are missing, building identity deterministically selects a weighted regional material and muted finish. British defaults favour slate; Mediterranean defaults favour clay; the temperate fallback mixes slate, clay and metal. These are coarse style assumptions, not surveyed roof identities or globally accurate architectural regions. Flat and inferred industrial buildings retain appropriate fallback surfaces. Tone variation is restrained and weathering is subtle. Material selection uses a separate random stream and does not change geometry, vegetation or roof fittings.

`roof_styles.py` is independent of Blender and has tests for mapped precedence, colours, deterministic identity and missing-data variation. `roof-finishes.json` in each generated area records material, colour, mapped/inferred flags and weathering per building part. `freeze.json` records the new generator's source hashes. The previous frozen experiments and their screenshots remain unchanged.

The same eleven photographic Poly Haven CC0 material sets are reused; no new downloads are needed for this change. Material attribution and original checksums remain in `assets/materials/batch-manifest.json`. Other sources, physics and limitations are described in `../batch_v2/README.md`: WorldCover/Meta-WRI classification and canopy, Overture/OSM geometry, and the RacingVibes vehicle snapshot. Areas remain 192 m square with inferred details and no hills, traffic, audio or live night lighting.
