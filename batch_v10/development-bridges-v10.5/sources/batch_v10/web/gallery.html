Bridges and terrain

Drive across fixed bridges and beneath overpasses. Connected road surfaces keep each route at its own height. Slopes affect acceleration and the scenery follows the terrain.

Bridge topology comes from mapping. Missing deck heights, approach shapes and bridge structures are inferred. Synthetic examples test the driving system and represent no real location.
