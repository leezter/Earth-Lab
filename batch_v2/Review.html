Earth Lab · Land cover and canopy, across all six settings

The same six areas, resolution, materials and lighting. Version 2 adds ESA WorldCover classification and modelled canopy heights, gathering neighbouring data cells. Roads and building footprints override coarse raster masks. Tree positions are generated within supported areas; they are not surveyed trunks. WorldCover is the 2021 v200 product, not a current street survey.

Version 1 is preserved unchanged. Version 2 is frozen before rendering and applied without individual scene repairs. These screenshots are automatic captures at matching camera settings. Terrain height, architectural detail and live lighting remain outside this version.

Tabernas countryside

Limited - class variation added; boundaries need blending

Ground is now divided into grass, sparse/bare and shrub areas, with low shrubs and no invented tall trees. Regional and seasonal material treatment is still needed; this is not accepted as a faithful Tabernas replica.
