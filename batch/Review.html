Earth Lab · Six settings, one frozen generator

Each scene uses the same frozen rules, 32 pixels per metre and 48 Cycles samples. Material selection follows mapped tags, building/land-use geometry and coarse regional fallbacks. No individual scene was manually repaired. Screenshots are automatic browser captures, at matching camera settings.

Technical generation success and visual acceptance are reported separately. Missing trees, unsuitable defaults, flattened hills and generic architecture are limitations, not proof of exact real-world reproduction. Build report
